Adarsh Digital Panel Features

A comprehensive administrative portal for schools and institutions to manage student records, delegate tasks, and order prints seamlessly.

Class-Wise Work Division

Schools, colleges, and organizations can create staff/assistant accounts and delegate work by assigning specific classes or courses to check, edit, and correct student data.

Flexible Reprint Requests

Easily request duplicate cards or reprints anytime due to loss, wear, or errors. Choose to print with or without corrections directly from the panel.

CBSE-Compliant Photos

Download high-quality, professionally edited student photos in compliant sizes and formats for easy upload to the CBSE portal, complete with custom name, date, class, or section text overlays.

Flexible Document Uploads

Import student records effortlessly in bulk. Support for Excel (.xlsx), Microsoft Word (.docx), or ZIP files containing student pictures and spreadsheets.

Excel-Style Inline Editing

Correct records instantly in a spreadsheet-like grid view. Perform fast inline cell edits directly on the portal without re-uploading documents.

Progress Tracking Dashboard

Monitor design, correction, and printing progress with a detailed statistics dashboard showing exact pending card volumes and how much work remains.
